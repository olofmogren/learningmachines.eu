About Learning Machines

Bridging machine learning research, applied AI, and climate solutions.

Learning Machines Seminars is an open platform dedicated to discussions in artificial intelligence and machine learning. We feature speakers ranging from foundational researchers to applied engineers.

Our sessions are hosted in Sweden (with hybrid participation in Gothenburg and Stockholm) and live-streamed freely to an open international community.

Co-Organizers

The seminar series is co-organized and supported by:

  • RISE (Research Institutes of Sweden): Sweden’s research institute and innovation partner supporting AI applications in society.
  • Climate AI Nordics: A regional network advancing machine learning methodologies for climate mitigation and adaptation.
